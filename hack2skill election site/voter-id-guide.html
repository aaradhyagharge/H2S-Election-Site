How to Register for Voter ID

मतदार नोंदणी मार्गदर्शक — एक पाऊल लोकशाहीकडे

Step-by-Step Guide (Form 6)

1

Check Eligibility

You must be an Indian citizen, 18 years or older as of the qualifying date, and a resident of the area.

2

Visit Official Portal

Go to voters.eci.gov.in or use the Voter Helpline App.

3

Fill Form 6

Select 'New Registration for General Electors' and fill in your details accurately.

4

Upload Documents

You will need a passport photo, age proof, and address proof.

Required Documents

  • Age Proof: Birth Certificate, Aadhar Card, PAN Card, or Passport.
  • Address Proof: Ration Card, Passport, Bank Passbook, or Utility Bills.
  • Photograph: Recent passport-sized color photo.

Voter ID FAQ

Can I register online? ▼
Yes, via the ECI Voter Portal or Voter Helpline App.
What if my name is missing? ▼
Use Form 6 to register again or Form 8 for corrections.
How long does it take? ▼
Usually 2-4 weeks after verification by the Booth Level Officer (BLO).
